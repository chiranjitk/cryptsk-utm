   998→   998→## 5. Index & Integrity Notes
   999→   999→
  1000→  1000→- Hot paths: `firewall_logs(tenantId,timestamp)`, `ips_events(tenantId,timestamp)`,
  1001→  1001→  `audit_log(tenantId,createdAt)`, `firewall_rules(tenantId,enabled,position)`.
  1002→  1002→- Log retention job (DATA_LIFECYCLE §4) deletes by timestamp in batches; no FK from logs to config tables (rows survive rule deletion — `ruleName`/`appName` are denormalized strings on purpose).
  1003→  1003→- Deletes: config objects RESTRICT when referenced (service layer checks logical refs before delete and returns `CONFLICT` with referencing rule names); children with FK cascade: reservations/leases (subnet), vpn_sessions (vpn), qos_rules (policy), group members.
  1004→  1004→- All list endpoints are tenant-scoped automatically (handler injects `tenantId` from JWT).
