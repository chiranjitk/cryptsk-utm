Cryptsk UTM · red edition

One palette, ten directions: white / red / dark red only — from crisp crimson minimalism and scarlet editorial print to oxblood luxury, cherry noir and a red-phosphor terminal. Every template renders the same dashboard (KPIs, traffic chart, threat mix, live events, system health) so you can compare them fairly. All built in real CSS: what you see is what your app becomes.

How to choose: scroll through all ten, then reply in chat with the number — e.g. “Template 6”. You can also mix and match: “Template 6, but with Template 1's rounded corners”, “Template 3 in light mode”, or describe any tweak (shade of red, fonts, corners, light/dark). Once you pick, I will convert the entire app — login screen, sidebar, all 20+ modules — to that design. Tip: every template has a dark AND a daylight version — flip the whole gallery with the mode buttons below.
