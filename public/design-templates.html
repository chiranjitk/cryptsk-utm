Cryptsk UTM · design gallery

Batch 2 (11–20) is built to your spec: pure #FFFFFF canvas on every template, one confident secondary color each — cobalt, violet, ink, rose, tangerine, magenta, cerulean, gold, blueprint-blue, cocoa — and zero green anywhere. Every Batch-2 template is also a different layout, not a recolor: broadsheet editorial, bento grid, Swiss mono index, claymorphism, icon rail, pop-art panels, floating dock, art-deco gilt, engineering blueprint and a master–detail split. Batch 1 (01–10, red edition) stays below for comparison — same dashboard data in all 20, so compare fairly. All real CSS: what you see is what your app becomes.

How to choose: scroll through all twenty, then reply in chat with the number — e.g. “Template 14”. You can also mix and match: “Template 12, but with Template 19's mono font”, “Template 17 in dark mode”, or describe any tweak (color, font, corners, density). Once you pick, I will convert the entire app — login screen, sidebar/topbar, all 20+ modules — to that design.
